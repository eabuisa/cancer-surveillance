  • NCCN Testicular Cancer v2.2024, TEST-A Table 1 (stage I seminoma surveillance after orchiectomy) and Table 2 (after adjuvant chemotherapy or radiation: H&P every 6–12 months years 1–2, then annually; CT/MRI annually years 1–3, none years 4–5).

  • NCCN Testicular Cancer v2.2024, TEST-B Table 6 (stage I NSGCT without risk factors, active surveillance) and Table 7 (with risk factors). Risk factors = lymphovascular invasion or invasion of the spermatic cord or scrotum; some centers also count predominant embryonal carcinoma.
  • After 1 cycle of adjuvant BEP or primary RPLND (TEST-B Table 8): H&P + markers every 3 months years 1–2, every 6 months years 3–4, annually year 5; abdomen ± pelvis CT/MRI annually years 1–2 only; chest X-ray every 6–12 months year 1, annually year 2.
  • Stage II–III NSGCT after complete response to chemotherapy ± post-chemotherapy RPLND (TEST-B Table 9): H&P + markers every 2 months year 1, every 3 months year 2, every 6 months years 3–5; abdomen ± pelvis CT/MRI every 6 months year 1, every 6–12 months year 2, annually year 3, then as clinically indicated.
  • Use the same imaging modality (CT or MRI) throughout surveillance; chest CT with contrast is preferred over X-ray when thoracic symptoms are present. Testicular ultrasound for any equivocal exam.

History & physical exam + tumor markersno scheduled interval beyond year 5 — follow-up may be extended beyond 5 years at physician discretion
Chest X-rayno scheduled interval beyond year 5
  • NCCN Testicular Cancer v2.2026, TEST-A Table 6 (bulky stage IIB, IIC, and stage III seminoma after chemotherapy). Unlike stage I/II seminoma, tumor markers are a standard (not optional) part of follow-up here.
  • VERSION CHANGE FLAGGED: v2.2024 Table 6 used fixed intervals (H&P + markers every 2 months year 1, every 3 months year 2, every 6 months years 3–4, annually year 5; chest X-ray every 2 then 3 months in years 1–2). v2.2026 widened years 1–2 to ranges (every 2–4 and 3–6 months) and relaxed chest X-ray to every 4 then 6 months. The v2.2026 values are used here; imaging cadence is confirmed by the 2026 HealthHelp CT policy.
  • Add chest CT if there was supradiaphragmatic disease at diagnosis. FDG-PET/CT (skull base to mid-thigh) as clinically indicated.
  • Residual mass >3 cm that is FDG-PET negative: MRI or CT every 6 months for the first year, then annually for 5 years (NCCN). Residual masses may require more frequent imaging.
  • CHANGE (stage IV audit): 'After year 5' band added. TEST-A Table 6 has NO years 6–10 column and NO annual-marker footnote for years 5–10 (unlike TEST-B Table 10 for nonseminoma). The band encodes only what NCCN states: footnote e ('CT or MRI is not recommended beyond 5 years unless clinically indicated') and the TEST-A preamble (follow-up 'may be extended beyond 5 years at the discretion of the physician'; reassess with any new/worsening symptoms). No scheduled H&P/marker or chest interval exists beyond year 5 — do not invent one.
