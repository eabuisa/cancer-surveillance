Vulvar Cancer — Surveillance & Follow-Up Schedule (NCCN)

Follow-up schedules for vulvar cancer based on NCCN guidelines, organized by stage and treatment path, then by years since treatment.

Test / examFrequency

  • Schedule per NCCN Vulvar Cancer (squamous cell carcinoma and adenocarcinoma), restated in Medscape and the American Cancer Society follow-up guidance (citing NCCN Vulvar Cancer v1.2025).
  • Cervical/vaginal cytology screening for lower genital tract neoplasia (may include HPV testing) is performed as indicated by symptoms or examination findings, not as routine recurrence surveillance.
  • Routine surveillance imaging and laboratory tests (CBC, BUN, creatinine) are NOT recommended; CT or PET/CT only if symptoms or examination suggest recurrence.
  • Consider FDG-PET/CT at 3–6 months to assess treatment response after definitive primary treatment (NCCN Vulvar Cancer v3.2024, VULVA-8).
  • Exception: stage IA disease treated with simple partial vulvectomy with negative margins may be followed with observation alone (NCCN).
  • Scheduled groin ultrasound surveillance is an NCCN element of the vulvar MELANOMA pathway, not vulvar SCC; some centers nonetheless consider groin ultrasound every 3 months in year 1 after sentinel node biopsy (institutional practice, e.g. Saskatchewan Cancer Agency) — not an NCCN recommendation for SCC.

Sources: reference.medscape.com · amp.cancer.org · fasgo.org.ar

Years 3–5

Test / examFrequency
Groin nodal ultrasound (stage ≥IB)every 6–12 months
CT scan / FDG-PET/CTconsider every 3–6 months (VM-3 states no separate later-phase interval)
Full melanoma follow-upper NCCN Melanoma: Cutaneous follow-up recommendations (ME-10/ME-11)

After year 5

Test / examFrequency
Full melanoma follow-upper NCCN Melanoma: Cutaneous follow-up recommendations (ME-10/ME-11); VM-3 states no vulvovaginal-melanoma-specific items beyond year 5
  • Per NCCN Vulvar Cancer v2.2026, Vulvar and Vulvovaginal Melanoma — Follow-up/Surveillance (VM-3): groin nodal ultrasound is recommended for stage ≥IB every 3–6 months for the first 2 years, then every 6–12 months for years 3–5; consider CT every 3–6 months and FDG-PET/CT every 3–6 months, particularly in high-risk disease; otherwise see the follow-up recommendations in NCCN Melanoma: Cutaneous (ME-10/ME-11). Version 1.2026 tightened the CT and FDG-PET/CT intervals on VM-3 from every 3–12 months to every 3–6 months.
  • This variant also covers vaginal melanoma: the NCCN Vaginal Cancer guideline refers vulvovaginal melanoma to this same VM pathway, and vulvovaginal melanoma is staged the same as cutaneous melanoma (AJCC melanoma staging).
  • Nodal ultrasound assessment for melanoma requires specific radiologic expertise; suspicious nodes should undergo FNA/core biopsy directed at the atypical area of cortex (VM-3 footnote).
  • Source note: the CT and FDG-PET/CT cadence is independently corroborated by HealthHelp's 2026 imaging policy restating NCCN Vulvar v1.2026 ('CT pelvis every 3 to 6 months; FDG-PET/CT in cases of high-risk disease, every 3 to 6 months'). The groin-ultrasound cadence is stated verbatim in NCCN VM-3; no independent restatement of the ultrasound cadence was found.
  • This melanoma pathway is distinct from vulvar SCC surveillance: the 'consider groin ultrasound every 3 months in year 1 after sentinel node biopsy' item in the SCC variant's notes is institutional practice for SCC, not this NCCN melanoma recommendation.

Sources: dr2pp.oss.ns-svc.cn · www.healthhelp.com · reference-global.com

No local therapy (no surgery, no radiation)

  • The stage IVB pathway (VULVA-7) offers systemic therapy and/or best supportive care with no surveillance or monitoring schedule; NCCN follow-up for vulvar cancer (VULVA-8) applies after treatment only.
