  • No routine surveillance imaging: CT only if progression, bulky disease assessment, or Richter transformation is suspected. Repeat flow cytometry is not part of routine follow-up.
  • Treatment is initiated only when iwCLL active-disease criteria are met (progressive marrow failure, bulky/progressive adenopathy or splenomegaly, lymphocyte doubling time <6 months, autoimmune cytopenias unresponsive to steroids, significant B symptoms); a rising lymphocyte count alone is not an indication.
  • Monitor for autoimmune cytopenias (AIHA, ITP), infections, hypogammaglobulinemia, and second malignancies — skin cancer screening and age-appropriate cancer screening are particularly important.
  • Keep vaccinations current (influenza, pneumococcal, recombinant zoster, COVID-19); avoid live vaccines.
  • Post-treatment follow-up uses the same history/exam/CBC cadence, with additional monitoring for treatment-specific late toxicities.
  • Per NCCN CLL/SLL guidance (v1.2026 referenced): during watch-and-wait, CBC and physical exam are performed roughly every 3–12 months depending on disease stability; many practices use every 3–6 months.
