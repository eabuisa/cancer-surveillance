Soft Tissue Sarcoma — Surveillance & Follow-Up Schedule (NCCN)

Follow-up schedules for soft tissue sarcoma based on NCCN guidelines, organized by stage and treatment path, then by years since treatment.

  • NCCN phrases chest and primary-site imaging for extremity / superficial trunk disease as 'consider, based on histology / estimated risk of locoregional recurrence' rather than a fixed interval for the primary site; the stage-stratified chest intervals above are from NCCN restatements (stage I every 6–12 months; stage II/III every 3–6 months for the first 5 years, then annually).
  • CONFLICT on history & physical after years 2–3: the Medscape restatement of the JNCCN Version 2.2022 guideline gives H&P every 3–6 months for 2–3 years and then annually directly (for both stage I and stage II/III), omitting an every-6-months phase. The every-6-months-for-the-next-2-years phase used here is restated for NCCN by the Canadian Cancer Society (citing Soft Tissue Sarcoma Version 1.2025), the ECOG-ACRIN chest-surveillance trial summary of NCCN, and peer-reviewed case literature.
  • CONFLICT on stage II/III chest imaging in the first 2–3 years: one payer restatement of NCCN Version 1.2025 gives every 2–6 months, then every 6 months to complete 5 years, then annually; most other NCCN restatements give every 3–6 months. Every 3–6 months was used here.
  • No routine abdomen/pelvis surveillance is recommended for extremity, body wall, or head/neck soft tissue sarcoma — surveillance imaging is the chest and the primary site only.
  • After 10 years the likelihood of recurrence is small and follow-up should be individualized by subtype (NCCN, restated in Medscape / JNCCN Version 2.2022).
  • Rehabilitation / functional assessment is part of post-treatment follow-up in NCCN for extremity disease, but carries no fixed surveillance interval.
  • NCCN versions cited by sources: Soft Tissue Sarcoma Version 2.2022 (JNCCN, restated in Medscape), Version 5.2024 and Version 1.2025 (HealthHelp / payer imaging restatements), and Version 1.2025 (Canadian Cancer Society).

Sources: www.medscape.com · www.healthhelp.com · www.healthhelp.com · cancer.ca · jaypeedigital.com · www.centerwatch.com

Chest CT (as part of chest/abdomen/pelvis imaging)every 3–6 months

Years 3–5 (next 2 years)

Test / examFrequency
Physical examinationevery 6 months
CT or MRI abdomen/pelvis (primary site)every 6 months
Chest CT (as part of chest/abdomen/pelvis imaging)every 6 months

Beyond year 5

Test / examFrequency
Physical examinationannually
CT or MRI abdomen/pelvis (primary site)annually
Chest CT (as part of chest/abdomen/pelvis imaging)annually
  • NCCN follow-up for resected retroperitoneal / intra-abdominal sarcoma is a physical examination with imaging on the same cadence in all bands; the schedule is the same regardless of R0 / R1 / R2 margin status (adjuvant therapy choices differ, follow-up does not).
  • Imaging modality: CT or MRI — the payer restatement of NCCN Version 5.2024 adds 'consider PET/CT' for retroperitoneal surveillance imaging; PET/CT is not a routine-interval item in the JNCCN restatement.
  • CONFLICT on the length of the first band: the HealthHelp restatement of NCCN Soft Tissue Sarcoma Version 1.2025 gives chest/abdomen/pelvis CT or MRI every 3–6 months for 3 years, then every 6 months for the next 2 years, then annually, whereas the JNCCN Version 2.2022 restatement (Medscape), the Version 5.2024 payer restatement, and peer-reviewed case literature citing NCCN all give the first band as 2–3 years. The 2–3 year phrasing was used here as the majority / JNCCN wording.
  • Alberta Health Services' sarcoma follow-up guideline (citing NCCN Soft Tissue Sarcoma Version 1.2025) phrases retroperitoneal surveillance more simply as CT chest/abdomen/pelvis every 3–6 months for the first 2 years, then annual follow-up to year 5 — consistent on modality and early interval, shorter on the intermediate phase.
  • NCCN versions cited by sources: Soft Tissue Sarcoma Version 2.2022 (JNCCN, restated in Medscape and in Frontiers in Oncology case literature), Version 5.2024 and Version 1.2025 (HealthHelp / payer imaging restatements).

Sources: www.medscape.com · www.healthhelp.com · www.healthhelp.com · www.frontiersin.org · www.ahs.ca

Desmoid tumor (aggressive fibromatosis)

First 2–3 years

Test / examFrequency
CT or MRI (contrast-enhanced; MRI preferred)every 3–6 months

Thereafter

Test / examFrequency
CT or MRIevery 6–12 months
Ultrasoundmay be considered for select locations (e.g., abdominal wall) for long-term follow-up
  • Desmoid tumors are benign, non-metastasizing fibroblastic tumors covered in the NCCN Soft Tissue Sarcoma guideline; this is an imaging surveillance schedule (used both for active surveillance of unresected tumors and after treatment), not a post-resection cancer schedule — there is no chest surveillance and no fixed end date.
  • Conflict flag — length of the first phase: restatements of NCCN Soft Tissue Sarcoma Version 5.2024 and the 2025 FAP-desmoid review citing NCCN give every 3–6 months for 2–3 years, then every 6–12 months thereafter; some newer restatements (NCCN Version 5.2026, via a manufacturer guideline summary) phrase the first phase as 3 years. The 2–3 year phrasing is used here.
  • Contrast-enhanced imaging is preferred; for long-term surveillance, MRI may be substituted to minimize radiation exposure (NCCN imaging note restated in payer policies).

Sources: www.healthhelp.com · pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov

No local therapy (no surgery, no radiation)

  • For unresectable or stage IV soft tissue sarcoma, NCCN recommends imaging to assess treatment response (CT preferred, or MRI) and permits observation of asymptomatic indolent disease, but states no interval. (Desmoid tumors under active surveillance are covered by the desmoid pathway in this app.)
