Follow-up schedules for thymomas & thymic carcinoma based on NCCN guidelines, organized by stage and treatment path, then by years since treatment.

Years 6–10

Test / examFrequency
CT chest with contrast — thymomaannually (to year 10)
  • Per NCCN Thymomas and Thymic Carcinomas, Version 3.2025, as restated in 2025 surveillance reviews and imaging policies. Chest MRI is an acceptable alternative to CT in certain clinical situations (e.g., younger patients, to limit cumulative radiation).
  • Thymic carcinoma surveillance ends at year 5 per NCCN; thymoma surveillance runs to year 10, and NCCN notes the optimal duration has not been established — recurrences are documented well beyond 10 years, so many clinicians continue annual imaging beyond year 10 for thymoma.
  • Clinical follow-up should include review for new respiratory/constitutional symptoms and for myasthenia gravis or other autoimmune manifestations, which can accompany recurrence; no guideline specifies routine surveillance labs or PET (PET is generally not recommended for surveillance).

Sources: healthhelp.com · med.amegroups.org

Unresectable / advanced / metastatic — surveillance after systemic therapy (no resection)

Years 1-2

Test / examFrequency
CT chest with contrast — thymomaevery 6 months
CT chest with contrast — thymic carcinomaevery 3-6 months

After year 2

Test / examFrequency
CT chest with contrast — thymomaannually, until year 10
CT chest with contrast — thymic carcinomaannually, until year 5
  • Per NCCN Thymomas and Thymic Carcinomas, THYM-4 (Recurrent, Advanced, or Metastatic Disease): a single surveillance column applies to every THYM-4 treatment branch, including locally advanced unresectable disease treated with systemic therapy (with or without chemoradiation), medically inoperable/unresectable disease treated with systemic therapy or observation, solitary/pleural metastases treated with systemic therapy, and extrathoracic metastases treated with systemic therapy. Verbatim: 'Chest CT with contrast every 3-6 mo for thymic carcinoma and every 6 mo for thymoma for 2 y, then annually until 5 y for thymic carcinoma and until 10 y for thymoma.'
  • This is post-treatment surveillance for advanced disease, not an on-therapy response-assessment interval: NCCN states no per-cycle restaging interval for thymic malignancies on systemic therapy (THYM-C).
  • Chest MRI is an acceptable alternative to CT in certain clinical situations (e.g., younger patients, to limit cumulative radiation) — THYM-4 footnote l.
  • NCCN footnote m: 'The duration for surveillance has not been established.' Intervals verified in Version 1.2024 (THYM-4) and unchanged in substance in Version 2.2026 (April 10, 2026) Discussion and the 2025 NCCN summary in the AME surveillance review.
  • Evaluate clinically at follow-up for signs of myasthenia gravis and other paraneoplastic syndromes (THYM-4 footnote a). No routine surveillance labs or PET are specified.
  • Intervals are numerically identical to the post-resection variant's R1/R2-or-locally-advanced row because THYM-3's surveillance bullet explicitly states it '(also applies to THYM-4)'; this variant records the THYM-4 (never-resected) application separately.

Sources: thymic.org · dr2pp.oss.ns-svc.cn · www.healthhelp.com
