After treatment — pT0 / pT1 (renal pelvis or ureter)

Year 1

Test / examFrequency
Cystoscopyevery 3 months
Urine cytologyconsider at similar intervals (every 3 months) for high-grade tumors
Upper tract imaging or ureteroscopy — only if nephron-sparing surgeryevery 3–12 months
Abdomen/pelvis CT or MRI — only if nephron-sparing surgery± at 3–12-month intervals

After year 1

Test / examFrequency
Cystoscopy (± cytology)at longer intervals — NCCN does not specify the interval or a stop point
Upper tract imaging / ureteroscopy — if nephron-sparing surgerycontinue long-term (>5 years), with urine cytology and cross-sectional urography or endoscopic visualization
  • NCCN Bladder Cancer v1.2026, Upper GU Tract Tumors follow-up (UTT-4) — a single follow-up table covers renal pelvis and ureter primaries, separate from the bladder follow-up table (BL-E). Confirmed against the Curr Opin Urol 2025 guideline comparison (NCCN column) and the guideline-of-guidelines review.
  • After radical nephroureterectomy there is no remaining upper tract to image on the treated side; the 3–12-month upper-tract imaging/endoscopy items apply to nephron-sparing management (segmental resection, endoscopic ablation, chemoablation).
  • NCCN discussion: long-term surveillance (>5 years), including urine cytology and cross-sectional urography or endoscopic visualization, is required after nephron-sparing treatment because of the high recurrence risk.

After treatment — pT2 / pT3 / pT4 or pN+ (renal pelvis or ureter)

Year 1

Test / examFrequency
Cystoscopy + urine cytologyevery 3 months
Upper tract imaging or ureteroscopy — only if nephron-sparing surgeryevery 3–12 months
Abdomen/pelvis CT or MRI + chest imaging — only if nephron-sparing surgeryat 3–12-month intervals

After year 1

Test / examFrequency
Cystoscopy + urine cytologyat longer intervals — NCCN does not specify the interval or a stop point
  • NCCN Bladder Cancer v1.2026, UTT-4: pT2, pT3, pT4, or pN+ tumors are followed the same as pT0/pT1 with the ADDITION of chest imaging (in the nephron-sparing imaging set) — cytology is routine here rather than 'consider for high grade' as in pT0/pT1.
  • Adjuvant treatment context for this group (UTT-4): adjuvant platinum-based chemotherapy is discussed for pT3/pT4/pN+ without prior neoadjuvant therapy; adjuvant nivolumab or pembrolizumab is considered for pT2–pT4/pN+ (or ypT2–ypT4/ypN+ after neoadjuvant therapy). Follow-up above applies after completion of primary ± adjuvant treatment.
  • Confirmed against the NCCN Bladder Cancer discussion text for upper tract tumors (123dok mirror of the JNCCN discussion) and the Curr Opin Urol 2025 / guideline-of-guidelines restatements of the NCCN follow-up.
