About this tool

Cancer Surveillance Guidelines summarizes published NCCN-guideline-based follow-up recommendations after cancer treatment — history and exam intervals, imaging, labs, and tumor markers — organized by disease site, stage or treatment path where the guidelines branch, and time since treatment. The schedules were compiled in November 2026, and each one was verified against at least two published sources (listed at the bottom of every schedule).

For clinician reference only. Verify against the current NCCN Clinical Practice Guidelines before acting; surveillance should be individualized to the patient. This tool does not replace the guideline or clinical judgment.
