  • Same follow-up applies after a complete or partial response to therapy and during observation (watch-and-wait) of untreated advanced-stage disease, per the NCCN B-Cell Lymphomas follow-up recommendations and Guidelines for Patients (v4.2026).
  • Watch for histologic transformation (~2–3% per year): rapidly enlarging nodes, new B symptoms, or rising LDH should prompt re-biopsy rather than imaging alone.
  • Conflict flagged: a 2026 literature review restates NCCN FL follow-up as clinical evaluation every 3–6 months for 5 years with imaging reserved for symptom-directed assessment only. The scheduled-CT schedule shown here (every 6 months for up to 2 years, then annually) follows the NCCN Guidelines for Patients and published restatements of the NCCN B-Cell follow-up table; imaging intensity should be individualized.
