  • Follow-up intensity is set by NCCN risk group (low / high / very high risk for local disease; separate, tighter schedule after regional nodal disease), per NCCN Squamous Cell Skin Cancer (Version 1.2023 risk framework, current restatements).
  • Routine surveillance imaging is NOT recommended; imaging (nodal-basin ultrasound, CT, PET/CT, or MRI) only if the clinical exam is insufficient to follow the disease or recurrence is suspected.
  • Every visit: patient education on sun protection and monthly self-examination of skin and (for high-risk/regional disease) lymph nodes.
  • Shorter intervals are appropriate for patients with multiple lesions or immunosuppression (e.g., solid-organ transplant recipients), who often warrant lifelong intensified surveillance.
  • 30–50% of patients develop another keratinocyte carcinoma within 5 years — the complete skin exam at each visit screens for new primaries as well as recurrence.
