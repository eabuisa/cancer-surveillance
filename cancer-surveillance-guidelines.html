About this tool

Cancer Surveillance Guidelines summarizes published NCCN-guideline-based follow-up recommendations — history and exam intervals, imaging, labs, and tumor markers — organized by disease site, stage or treatment path where the guidelines branch, and time since treatment. Alongside post-treatment surveillance, it includes monitoring for stage IV and metastatic disease where NCCN publishes a schedule: response assessment on systemic therapy, and follow-up after stage IV disease is resected or treated to no evidence of disease. Where NCCN publishes no separate stage IV schedule for a site, the pathway's notes say so rather than substitute another site's cadence. The schedules were compiled in November 2026, and each one was verified against at least two published sources (listed at the bottom of every schedule).

For clinician reference only. Verify against the current NCCN Clinical Practice Guidelines before acting; surveillance should be individualized to the patient. This tool does not replace the guideline or clinical judgment.
