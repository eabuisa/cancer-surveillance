Vaginal Cancer — Surveillance & Follow-Up Schedule (NCCN)

Follow-up schedules for vaginal cancer based on NCCN guidelines, organized by stage and treatment path, then by years since treatment.

After treatment

Years 1–2

Test / examFrequency
Interval history & physical exam (incl. pelvic exam)every 3–6 months
Post-treatment imaging (response assessment)once, at 3–4 months after completion of treatment
Cervical/vaginal cytology screening (may include HPV testing)consider annually, as indicated for detection of lower genital tract neoplasia (see note)
Laboratory assessment (CBC, blood urea nitrogen [BUN], creatinine)as indicated — based on symptoms or examination findings suspicious for recurrence

Years 3–5

Test / examFrequency
Interval history & physical exam (incl. pelvic exam)every 6–12 months
Cervical/vaginal cytology screening (may include HPV testing)consider annually, as indicated for detection of lower genital tract neoplasia (see note)

After year 5

Test / examFrequency
Interval history & physical examannually, based on risk of recurrence
Laboratory assessment (CBC, blood urea nitrogen [BUN], creatinine)as indicated — based on symptoms or examination findings suspicious for recurrence
Cervical/vaginal cytology screening (may include HPV testing)consider annually, as indicated for detection of lower genital tract neoplasia (see note)
  • Per NCCN Vaginal Cancer, Version 2.2026 (JNCCN 2026;24(3)); the panel endorses the updated SGO post-treatment surveillance recommendations, tailored to recurrence risk and patient preference.
  • Early post-treatment imaging (~3–4 months after chemoradiation) is for response assessment; thereafter, imaging only as indicated by symptoms or examination findings suspicious for recurrence.
  • Annual cervical/vaginal cytology can be considered for detection of lower genital tract dysplasia; its value in detecting recurrent vaginal cancer is limited, and prior radiation increases the false-positive rate.
  • Suspected recurrence should be proven by biopsy before treatment planning.
