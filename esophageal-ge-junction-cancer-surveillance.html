Esophageal / GE Junction Cancer — Surveillance & Follow-Up Schedule (NCCN)

After year 5

Test / examFrequency

  • Applies to T1b or greater, any N, or T1a N+ disease treated by esophagectomy, with or without perioperative/adjuvant therapy, and to trimodality therapy (neoadjuvant chemotherapy or chemoradiation followed by esophagectomy): CT every 6 months for up to 2 years, then annually up to 5 years in both NCCN-based imaging summaries.
  • Histology/stage nuance: surveillance is stage- and histology-specific in NCCN (separate squamous cell carcinoma and adenocarcinoma follow-up pages, ESOPH-9 and ESOPH-20). Early Tis/T1a N0 disease after esophagectomy is different — imaging studies for surveillance are not recommended and EGD is scheduled every 3 months in year 1, every 6 months in year 2, then annually indefinitely in the NCCN-based summaries; do not apply the T1b+ CT schedule to Tis/T1a.
  • Adenocarcinoma review restating NCCN gives CT chest/abdomen/pelvis with contrast every 6 months for up to 2 years after definitive therapy — sources differ on whether pelvis is routinely included vs as clinically indicated; clinician NCCN footnotes state pelvis CT if clinically indicated.
  • If Barrett esophagus was incompletely resected/ablated: EGD every 3 months for the first year, every 6 months for the second year, then annually indefinitely (NCCN-based summaries).
  • Routine PET/CT is not part of scheduled surveillance; PET/CT only when clinically indicated, e.g. for inconclusive or inadequate conventional imaging (Eviti/NCCN-based imaging guideline, effective 2026).
  • Routine esophageal/GE junction cancer-specific surveillance is not recommended for more than 5 years after end of treatment; additional follow-up after 5 years may be considered based on risk factors and comorbidities.
  • NCCN versions cited by sources: Esophageal and Esophagogastric Junction Cancers Version 3.2025 (HealthHelp CT guideline) and Version 3.2024 (eviCore summary, 26 Apr 2024). General H&P interval (every 3–6 months years 1–2, every 6–12 months years 3–5, then annually) is from the NCCN guideline discussion restated in the full-text guideline summary and corroborated by American Cancer Society follow-up guidance.

After year 5

Test / examFrequency
History & physicalannually
CT chest/abdomen with contrastnot routinely recommended after 5 years; as clinically indicated
Upper GI endoscopy (EGD)as clinically indicated
  • Applies to pretreatment T1b–T4/T4b, N0–N+ disease treated with definitive chemoradiation without esophagectomy, per NCCN-based summaries: CT every 3–6 months for the first 2 years and annually up to 5 years; EGD every 3–6 months for the first 2 years, then annually for 3 more years.
  • Conflict flagged: an ACS case-review restatement of NCCN Version 1.2022 describes definitive chemoradiation surveillance as contrast CT chest/abdomen every 6 months for up to 2 years (not every 3–6 months) with EGD every 3–6 months, and does not state the annual CT/EGD extension to year 5. The more current NCCN-based imaging summaries (Versions 2.2025 and 3.2025) were used here.
  • Definitive chemoradiation is used predominantly for squamous cell carcinoma and for patients not undergoing surgery; NCCN keeps separate squamous and adenocarcinoma follow-up pages — if histology-specific pages differ for an individual patient, follow the histology page rather than this combined variant.
  • Routine PET/CT is not part of scheduled surveillance; PET/CT only when clinically indicated, e.g. for inconclusive conventional imaging.
  • Routine cancer-specific surveillance is not recommended for more than 5 years after end of treatment; additional follow-up after 5 years may be considered based on risk factors and comorbidities.
  • NCCN versions cited by sources: Esophageal and Esophagogastric Junction Cancers Version 2.2025 (HealthHelp MRI guideline) and Version 3.2025 (HealthHelp CT guideline).

Sources: www.healthhelp.com · www.healthhelp.com · www.Facs.Org · www.cancer.org

Cross-sectional imaging (CT)not routine — as clinically indicated only

Year 2

Test / examFrequency
Upper GI endoscopy (EGD)every 6 months
Cross-sectional imaging (CT)not routine — as clinically indicated only

After year 2

Test / examFrequency
Upper GI endoscopy (EGD)annually, indefinitely
Cross-sectional imaging (CT)not routine — as clinically indicated only
  • Applies to Tis or T1a N0 disease treated by endoscopic resection or ablation. NCCN keeps separate histology-specific follow-up pages (ESOPH-9 squamous cell carcinoma, ESOPH-20 adenocarcinoma), but the EGD ladder is the same for both histologies in the NCCN-based summaries.
  • The identical EGD ladder applies to Tis/T1a N0 after esophagectomy and to incompletely resected/ablated Barrett esophagus — the schedule follows the early lesion, not the treatment modality.
  • T1b N0 after endoscopic resection/ablation is a different, more intensive schedule in NCCN: CT chest/abdomen with contrast may be considered every 6 months for the first 2 years, then annually for up to 5 years, plus EGD every 3 months in year 1, every 4–6 months in year 2, then annually indefinitely (NCCN-based imaging summaries, Versions 3.2023–2.2025).
  • NCCN states no fixed history & physical visit cadence and no routine surveillance imaging specific to this early group; routine surveillance imaging is not recommended (J Clin Med 2026 review of NCCN) — visits and imaging are individualized / as clinically indicated.
  • The usual NCCN statement that routine cancer-specific surveillance is not recommended beyond 5 years does not apply to this EGD ladder, which NCCN phrases as continuing annually indefinitely.
  • NCCN versions cited by sources: Esophageal and Esophagogastric Junction Cancers Version 2.2025 and Version 3.2023 (HealthHelp imaging guidelines restating NCCN surveillance), corroborated by the 2026 J Clin Med post-treatment surveillance review.
