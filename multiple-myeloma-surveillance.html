  • Per NCCN Multiple Myeloma follow-up/surveillance (MYEL-4/MYEL-5), Version 3.2024; the panel and 3–6 month interval are retained in Version 5.2026 (Jan 2026), which revised the 24-hour urine studies to 'as needed / as clinically indicated if abnormal at baseline or if FLC levels change significantly'.
  • Relapsed / progressive disease: NCCN specifies no separate monitoring cadence. The relapse pathway (MYEL-6, Version 5.2026) directs follow-up with this same MYEL-4 test list — footnote dd: 'Follow up with the tests listed on MYEL-4 under Follow-up/Surveillance' — so patients on therapy for relapsed/refractory myeloma are monitored with this panel at the same 3–6 month interval; regimen-specific toxicity labs and response checks are set by the treatment protocol, not by a distinct NCCN surveillance schedule.
  • Patients on active maintenance therapy are typically seen and labbed more often per the treatment protocol; the 3–6 month interval is the NCCN surveillance floor for follow-up after primary therapy.
  • MRD testing after therapy: consider as indicated for prognostication, after shared decision-making with the patient.
  • Plain skeletal survey is not the preferred surveillance modality when whole-body low-dose CT, PET/CT, or MRI is available.
