  • IMPORTANT: NCCN Mesothelioma: Pleural (Version 2.2025) does NOT specify a routine surveillance imaging schedule after treatment — imaging-policy restatements of NCCN list pleural mesothelioma surveillance as 'no imaging surveillance suggested', and eviCore's evidence review likewise notes NCCN offers no guidance on this topic. ESMO advises CT surveillance without a specific timeframe.
  • The intervals shown are common institutional practice (e.g., University of Maryland Greenebaum Comprehensive Cancer Center post-surgical protocol: CT chest every 3 months for 2 years, then every 6 months, then annually from year 5), not an NCCN schedule — individualize to treatment received and clinical status.
  • CT chest (with abdomen as clinically indicated) at any time for new signs or symptoms of recurrence/progression.
  • Do not confuse with NCCN mesothelioma: peritoneal, which does have a specified schedule (CT chest/abdomen/pelvis every 3–6 months for 5 years, then annually to year 10) — a different guideline.

Sources: www.healthhelp.com · www.evicore.com · www.umms.org

  • Per NCCN Mesothelioma: Peritoneal, Version 2.2026 (PEM-B, recommended surveillance) — a SEPARATE guideline from pleural mesothelioma, for which NCCN specifies no routine surveillance schedule (see the pleural variant). Do not apply the pleural 'no schedule' position to peritoneal disease.
  • Minor operational difference flagged: eviCore's NCCN-derived policy supports CT chest/abdomen/pelvis every 3 months for 2 years, then annually until year 10; the NCCN text itself (shown here) is every 3–6 months for 5 years, then yearly, with no stated stop.
