  • Complete response should be documented with FDG-PET/CT reverting to negative within 3 months of completing therapy. Routine surveillance imaging in an asymptomatic patient in CR is not recommended — imaging only for significant clinical concern for relapse or per protocol; FDG-PET/CT only if the last scan was Deauville 4–5, to confirm CR.
  • Late-effects screening (after year 5, lifelong): annual breast screening with mammography AND MRI for women irradiated to the chest (start 8–10 years post-treatment or age 40, whichever is earlier), with breast exam 1–2 times yearly; cardiovascular monitoring (annual blood pressure, lipids, consider ECHO/stress test every 5–10 years, carotid ultrasound every 10 years after neck RT); annual thyroid function testing after neck irradiation; monitor for second malignancies and cardiovascular disease in coordination with primary care (see NCCN Survivorship).
  • Annual influenza vaccination recommended; provide a treatment summary at completion of therapy.
  • ECHO at 1 year after completion of anthracycline therapy was added in NCCN Hodgkin Lymphoma v1.2026 (HODG-12 update).
  • Per NCCN Hodgkin Lymphoma HODG-12/HODG-12A (v2.2024, updates through v1.2026 noted); NCCN Guidelines for Patients based on v2.2025/v2.2026.
