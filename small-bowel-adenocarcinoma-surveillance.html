  • Total surveillance duration is 5 years after resection; no routine surveillance schedule beyond year 5 is specified.
  • FDG-PET/CT is not indicated for surveillance.
  • NCCN footnote: no studies have been performed to assess ideal surveillance intervals for small bowel adenocarcinoma; colorectal cancer surveillance data are generally accepted as appropriate for SBA.
  • More intensive surveillance, which may include endoscopy/enteroscopy, may be needed for patients with Crohn disease / inflammatory bowel disease or familial syndromes (e.g., Lynch syndrome, familial adenomatous polyposis, Peutz-Jeghers syndrome).
  • NCCN version note: frequencies verified in the NCCN Small Bowel Adenocarcinoma clinician guideline Version 2.2026 (April 7, 2026) and in the 2026 NCCN Guidelines for Patients (Guide 3, 'Surveillance after surgery'); identical frequencies appear in Version 1.2020 and in payer-policy restatements of Versions 2.2025/3.2025, so this schedule is not new in 2025–2026.
  • Minor wording variation between sources: the clinician guideline lists 'CEA and CA 19-9' on the SBA-6/SBA-7 surveillance columns, while other pages and older versions phrase it 'CEA and/or CA 19-9'; both markers are listed in the NCCN patient guideline Guide 3.

No local therapy (no surgery, no radiation)

  • NCCN surveillance for small bowel adenocarcinoma is written for after resection. For metastatic disease on chemotherapy, NCCN names response and conversion-assessment imaging but states no interval; no monitoring schedule exists for never-resected disease.
