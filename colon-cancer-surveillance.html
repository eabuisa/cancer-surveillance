Colon Cancer — Surveillance & Follow-Up Schedule (NCCN)

Follow-up schedules for colon cancer based on NCCN guidelines, organized by stage and treatment path, then by years since treatment.

Stage I

After surgery

Test / examFrequency
Colonoscopyat 1 year after surgery; if advanced adenoma, repeat in 1 year; if no advanced adenoma, repeat in 3 years, then every 5 years
  • Colonoscopy is the only routine surveillance for stage I — no routine history/physical surveillance schedule, CEA, or CT chest/abdomen/pelvis is recommended (low recurrence risk).
  • No routine FDG-PET/CT for surveillance.
  • ctDNA is not recommended for surveillance (NCCN footnote, Colon Cancer v3.2025 COL-8).
  • Advanced adenoma is defined in the guideline footnote as villous polyp, polyp >1 cm, or high-grade dysplasia.
  • NCCN version note: verified in NCCN Colon Cancer clinician guideline Version 3.2025 (April 24, 2025), COL-8 surveillance table, and restated in the NCCN Guidelines for Patients: Colon Cancer, 2025 (based on Version 4.2025) and in payer-policy restatements of NCCN Colon Cancer Versions 1.2025/5.2024.

Sources: united-surgeons.com · pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov · pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov · www.healthhelp.com

Stage II–III

Years 1–2

Test / examFrequency
History and physical examinationevery 3–6 months
CEAevery 3–6 months
CT chest/abdomen/pelvisevery 6–12 months, for a total of 5 years from date of surgery
Colonoscopyat 1 year after surgery (at 3–6 months if no complete preoperative colonoscopy due to obstructing lesion); if advanced adenoma, repeat in 1 year; if no advanced adenoma, repeat in 3 years, then every 5 years

Years 3–5

Test / examFrequency
History and physical examinationevery 6 months, for a total of 5 years
CEAevery 6 months, for a total of 5 years
CT chest/abdomen/pelvisevery 6–12 months, for a total of 5 years from date of surgery
Colonoscopyper 1-year findings — if no advanced adenoma, repeat at 3 years, then every 5 years; if advanced adenoma, repeat in 1 year
  • No routine FDG-PET/CT — PET/CT is not indicated for surveillance (COL-8).
  • ctDNA is not recommended for surveillance (NCCN footnote, Colon Cancer v3.2025 COL-8); de-escalation of care based on ctDNA is not recommended.
  • Routine CEA monitoring and CT scanning are not recommended beyond 5 years.
  • CEA surveillance is intended for patients who are potential candidates for further intervention (e.g., resection of isolated metastases).
  • CT frequency note: in COL-8 the every 6–12 month CT interval is category 2B for frequency <12 months.
  • NCCN version note: verified in NCCN Colon Cancer clinician guideline Version 3.2025 (April 24, 2025), COL-8; identical schedule restated for NCCN Colon Cancer Version 4.2025 in the PRODIGE-13 review and for the 2025 NCCN guidelines in World J Gastroenterol 2025.

Sources: united-surgeons.com · pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov · oncodaily.com · www.healthhelp.com

Stage IV, resected — no evidence of disease

Years 1–2

Test / examFrequency
History and physical examinationevery 3–6 months
CEAevery 3–6 months
CT chest/abdomen/pelvisevery 3–6 months for 2 years
Colonoscopyat 1 year after surgery (at 3–6 months if no complete preoperative colonoscopy due to obstructing lesion); if advanced adenoma, repeat in 1 year; if no advanced adenoma, repeat in 3 years, then every 5 years
Colonoscopyinitiate from first documentation of complete clinical response (stage IV disease managed nonoperatively with complete clinical response)

Years 3–5

Test / examFrequency
History and physical examinationevery 6 months, for a total of 5 years
CEAevery 6 months, for a total of 5 years
CT chest/abdomen/pelvisevery 6–12 months, for a total of 5 years
Colonoscopyper 1-year findings — if no advanced adenoma, repeat at 3 years, then every 5 years; if advanced adenoma, repeat in 1 year
  • Applies after curative-intent resection (primary and/or metastasectomy) with no evidence of disease; surveillance is the same as stage II–III except CT is more frequent in the first 2 years.
  • No routine FDG-PET/CT — PET/CT is not indicated for surveillance (COL-8).
  • ctDNA is not recommended for surveillance (NCCN footnote, Colon Cancer v3.2025 COL-8).
  • Routine CEA monitoring and CT scanning are not recommended beyond 5 years.
  • CEA surveillance is intended for patients who are potential candidates for further intervention.
  • CT frequency note: in COL-8 the every 3–6 month CT interval in years 1–2 is category 2B for frequency <6 months.
  • NCCN version note: verified in NCCN Colon Cancer clinician guideline Version 3.2025 (April 24, 2025), COL-8 stage IV row; corroborated by the stage IV CRC review in PMC (H&P/CEA every 3–6 months x 2 years, then every 6 months to 5 years; CT every 3–6 months x 2 years, then every 6–12 months to 5 years; colonoscopy at 1 year, then 3 years, then every 5 years) and by World J Gastroenterol 2025 restating NCCN 2025.

Sources: united-surgeons.com · pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov · pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov · www.mdpi.com

Unresectable / metastatic, never resected — monitoring on systemic therapy (no local therapy)

On systemic therapy — potentially convertible metastatic disease

Test / examFrequency
Re-evaluation for conversion to resectability (cross-sectional imaging reassessment)every 2 months

On immunotherapy (MSI-H/dMMR disease)

Test / examFrequency
Re-evaluation of disease statusevery 2–3 months
  • NCCN's surveillance table (COL-8) applies only after resection (stages I, II–III, and resected stage IV rendered NED). NCCN publishes no surveillance schedule for colon cancer that was never resected; the intervals shown are re-evaluation points stated in the NCCN Colon Cancer treatment pathways for unresected disease, not a surveillance table.
  • The every-2-month re-evaluation applies to patients with potentially convertible metastases being assessed for conversion to resectability; the every 2–3 month re-evaluation is stated on the immunotherapy pathways for locally advanced/metastatic MSI-H/dMMR disease (e.g. dostarlimab, nivolumab ± ipilimumab, pembrolizumab pathways: 're-evaluate disease status every 2–3 mo').
  • Do not extrapolate the post-resection COL-8 surveillance intervals (H&P/CEA every 3–6 months, CT every 6–12 months) to unresected disease on therapy.
