Abdominal CT/MRI or contrast-enhanced US — after ablative therapyat 1–3, 6, and 12 months, then annually
Chest X-ray or CT — after ablative therapyannually, for 5 years

After year 5

Test / examFrequency
History & physical exam + laboratory testsannually, as clinically indicated
Abdominal / chest imagingas clinically indicated
  • NCCN Kidney Cancer (KID-C; v2.2025, restated in NCCN v1.2026 by the Kidney Cancer Association): stage I post-nephrectomy — H&P and labs annually; baseline abdominal CT/MRI within 3–12 months, then annually for up to 5 years; chest imaging annually for at least 5 years.
  • Version note: older NCCN summaries (e.g., Medscape) listed post-nephrectomy abdominal imaging annually for 3 years; the current version extends annual imaging to up to 5 years — the current value is used here.
  • Ablative-therapy imaging at 1–3, 6, and 12 months then annually reflects the NCCN v2.2025 KID-C revision (older versions used a 3–6-month first scan).
  • Consider a more rigorous imaging schedule/modality for positive margins or adverse pathologic features (sarcomatoid differentiation, grade 3/4).

Sources: www.kidneycancer.org · emedicine.medscape.com · pdfs.semanticscholar.org

  • Conflict flagged: Medscape's NCCN summary groups stages II and III together (H&P every 3–6 months for 3 years; CMP every 6 months for 2 years). The stage-specific KID-C table — restated by the Kidney Cancer Association (NCCN v1.2026) and a 2025 peer-reviewed review — lists stage II separately: H&P and labs annually, abdominal CT/MRI every 6 months for 2 years then annually to 5 years, chest imaging annually for at least 5 years. The stage-specific schedule is used here.
  • Stage III schedule per NCCN KID-C: H&P every 3–6 months for 3 years, then annually up to 5 years; CMP every 6 months for 2 years, then annually up to 5 years; baseline abdominal and chest imaging within 3–6 months, then every 3–6 months for at least 3 years, then annually up to 5 years.
  • Pelvic imaging, head/spine MRI, and bone scan only as clinically indicated.

Stage IV / relapsed / unresectable — monitoring on systemic therapy

Ongoing, while on systemic therapy / observation

Test / examFrequency
H&PEvery 6–16 weeks for patients receiving systemic therapy, or more frequently as clinically indicated, adjusted for type of systemic therapy
Laboratory evaluationAs per requirements for the therapeutic agent being used
CT or MRI chest/abdomen/pelvis (with and without IV contrast, unless contraindicated)Baseline pretreatment or prior to observation, then every 6–16 weeks (per physician discretion, clinical status and therapeutic schedule; adjust shorter or longer by rate of disease change and sites of active disease)
MRI (preferred) or CT of headConsider at baseline, then as clinically indicated; annual surveillance scans at physician discretion
MRI of spineAs clinically indicated
Bone scanAs clinically indicated
  • Monitoring during therapy/observation, NOT post-treatment surveillance.
  • NCCN publishes NO separate follow-up table for stage IV disease resected to NED (e.g., after metastasectomy) — the only exception is indirect: patients who received adjuvant therapy after complete resection follow the stage III schedule (see stage4-ned-adjuvant). No schedule is given for metastasectomy/SBRT to NED without adjuvant therapy.
  • No single follow-up plan is appropriate for all patients; follow-up should be individualized based on treatment schedules, side effects, comorbidities and symptoms (KID-C footnote).
  • VA Kidney Pathway V4.2026 simplifies the imaging interval to 'baseline, then every 12 weeks,' inside the NCCN 6–16-week range; the NCCN range is retained here.

Sources: urology.wiki · pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov · emedicine.staging.medscape.com · www.cancer.va.gov

After year 5

Test / examFrequency
Clinical + laboratory evaluationannually; imaging as clinically indicated
  • ROUTING: NCCN publishes NO separate follow-up table for stage IV RCC resected to NED. KID-C states that patients who received adjuvant therapy should receive clinical follow-up as for stage III disease, and KID-3/KID-4 offer adjuvant pembrolizumab after complete resection of M1 disease (metastasectomy ± nephrectomy). This variant applies the KID-C stage III schedule to that subgroup only; schedule content is identical to the stage III rows of the Stage II–III variant.
  • Applies ONLY to M1-NED patients who received adjuvant therapy. For metastasectomy/SBRT to NED with NO adjuvant therapy, NCCN gives no follow-up schedule — undecided in the audit and deliberately not encoded.
  • Pelvic imaging, head/spine MRI, and bone scan only as clinically indicated (as for stage III).
