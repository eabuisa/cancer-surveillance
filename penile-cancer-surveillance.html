  • NCCN Penile Cancer v1.2025, Surveillance Schedule (PN-8): the table splits follow-up by BOTH primary-tumor treatment and nodal status. Penile-sparing (topical/local) therapy: exam every 3 months years 1–2, every 6 months years 3–5, annually years 5–10. Penectomy: exam every 6 months years 1–2, then annually years 3–10.
  • The PN-8 nodal row groups pN0 with pN1 for imaging (CT abdomen/pelvis + chest X-ray every 6 months years 1–2, annually years 3–4); it applies to patients whose nodes were pathologically staged — imaging is otherwise symptom/exam-driven in node-negative disease.
  • Consider inguinal ultrasound, CT, or MRI if the clinical exam is abnormal, the patient has obesity, or there was prior inguinal surgery. No routine labs are specified.

  • NCCN Penile Cancer v1.2025, PN-8: pN0/pN1 — clinical exam with CT abdomen/pelvis and chest X-ray every 6 months years 1–2, then every 12 months years 3–4. pN2/pN3 — every 3 months in year 1, then every 6 months years 2–4 (chest CT used for pN2–3). Corroborated by payer policy summarizing NCCN Penile Cancer v1.2024.

No local therapy (no surgery, no radiation)

  • The only follow-up schedule in the NCCN penile cancer guideline (PN-8) is defined after local treatment and by nodal status. For unresectable or metastatic disease on systemic therapy, NCCN lists response-assessment imaging modalities (PN-E) but states no frequency.
