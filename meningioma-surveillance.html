  • Per NCCN Central Nervous System Cancers, Version 2.2026 (BRAIN-A, Principles of Brain and Spine Tumor Imaging): the same schedule covers WHO grade 1, WHO grade 2, and unresected meningiomas under observation — NCCN does not split surveillance by grade 1 vs 2 or by extent of resection.
  • VERSION CHANGE / conflict flagged: earlier NCCN versions (2023, as quoted in the 2025 Neuro-Oncology Practice surveillance review) scheduled first-year scans at 3, 6, and 12 months; the current Version 2.2026 schedule is 1, 3, and 6 months. The current version is shown.
  • More frequent imaging may be required for meningiomas treated for recurrence or receiving systemic therapy.
  • NCCN sets no stop point for meningioma surveillance; late recurrences beyond 10 years are well documented, so long-term periodic imaging continues as clinically indicated.

  • Per NCCN Central Nervous System Cancers, Version 2.2026 (BRAIN-A). Listed alongside the grade 3 recommendations, BRAIN-A also states: add PET-MRI every 3–6 months, and consider less frequent follow-up after 5–10 years; more frequent imaging may be required when treating recurrence or giving systemic therapy.
  • Do not confuse with the 2025 Neuro-Oncology Practice evidence-based framework, whose authors propose their own grade 3 cadence (MRI every 3–4 months for 2 years, then every 6 months indefinitely) — that is an author proposal, not the NCCN schedule shown here.
